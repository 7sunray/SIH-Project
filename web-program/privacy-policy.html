Privacy Policy

GovWatch field inspection app · Last updated October 2026

What this app does

GovWatch is a field-inspection workspace for government shelter homes and institutes. Authorised inspectors sign in, verify site GPS presence, complete checklists, capture photo/video evidence, and review AI-flagged anomalies.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, role and jurisdiction, stored on our secure application server to authenticate you.
  • Location: device GPS is read only while you use GPS verification, to confirm presence at a registered institute address.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you capture or upload inspection evidence.
  • Inspection data: checklists, reports, assignments and anomaly reviews you create in the course of duty.

How data is used and shared

Data is used solely for inspection administration within the responsible departments. We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or share data with third parties except the hosting infrastructure required to operate the service.

Security and retention

Sessions use short-lived tokens, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and sensitive fields are encrypted at rest. Records are retained per applicable government record schedules.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the system administrator at superadmin@dosje.gov.in.
